Beautiful view of the sea and port
Nice place for a short stroll from the pier to the bridge and over to Saeseom Island. We took quite a number of photos of the sea, bridge, and looking inland to Seogwipo Port. We enjoyed the sea breeze, the view of the clear waters and the displays of acrobatic jumping fish in the port. Locals stroll the area and it is a picturesque place to relax for a while. If you're into nature walks you can follow the trail around the island, which is probably small enough for you to complete in an hour or so.You can also take the submarine tour nearby at Seogwipo Port - but do check the weather conditions, our guide told us that the currents and weather play a big part in whether you'll be able to see lots of marine life during your dive.
