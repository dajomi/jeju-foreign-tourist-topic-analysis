Amazing
This is one of the biggest festival celebrated in Jeju island. Free shuttle buses were provided in various pick up point within Jeju City such as Top Dong station, Jeju City Hall station and Jeju University station and more.People of Jeju and tourists like me would gather around the oreum (hill) for the stage performances, fireworks and eventually the field burning event. It is definitely worth visit if you are in Jeju.
An amazing event!
There was so much to see and do, and the actual burning of the hill is spectacular. I spent half a day there and hadn’t seen everything. The thing to be aware of is the time it can take to get out of the car park afterwards, but totally worth it !
Tickets from $7.50
