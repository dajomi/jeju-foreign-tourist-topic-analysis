No waterfall but lovely walk
A lovely puppy greets you at the start of the walk! You walk past a lovely stone house on the left and the dry riverbed on the right.  They have well built stairs to the falls.  At this time of year there are no falls - due to lack of rain, however there is a lovely pond at the bottom and the ability to climb down the rock's and close to the water.  A lovely place to sit and contemplate!  Don't recommend kissing the kissing cave...a blarney stone it's not haha!
