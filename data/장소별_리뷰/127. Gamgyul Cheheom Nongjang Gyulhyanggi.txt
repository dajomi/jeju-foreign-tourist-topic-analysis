Fun & wonderful experience not to be missed while in Jeju.....
We wanted to visit an orange, (tangerine) farm in Jeju, where the famous Jeju oranges are grown.Through the tourists information centre, we found this farm nearest from the city centre. We had to take a bus.It is a very nice orange farm. Fee is 5,000₩ per pax- with unlimited time, eat as much oranges as you want while in the orchard & get to bring back 1kg of oranges at the end of it.Great deal considering 1 small tray of oranges in the markets are selling for same price or higher.The young lady in charge was very friendly & helpful although she don't speak English ( Google Translate comes in handy everytime ).The shop was very nice too. Products made at the farm using their fresh oranges includes jams, confectionery & oranges as well. The small production factory could be seen from the shop.Free tasting of their orange jam plus few pieces biscuits included. The jam was very good, made of real oranges.
