Some animals in inhumane conditions
When visiting other countries I realize the treatment of animal standards may differ, but I at least look for animals to be happy. This falconry “center”was bittersweet. Some of the animals had wonderful enclosures, but the birds of prey were treated very inhumanly. The same birds of prey/owls were tied to stands for the whole 3 hours we were there, with no food or water, in the blaring sun, with no supervision as people crowded and tried to touch them to the point they would flinch or try to fly away, only to be yanked by their chains. Their inhumane treatment brought tears to our eyes. At the far part of the center, we came upon their enclosures, cages about 5 x 5 x5 feet wide, where other birds of prey were chained, with little place to move and absolutely no enrichment toys.  This is unacceptable. There was a raccoon in a small enclosure with no enrichment toys, solely used for tourists to take selfies with. Last but not least, a pair of Coaty were also in a small enclosure. Their baby was on display in a small cage by the gift shop with no water. This “Center” does have some acceptable enclosures for fowl, rabbits, budgies, and the alpaca interaction was amazing, but surely they can treat all their animals with dignity. While we were there, a macaw flew away into the surrounding trees. Clearly the parrot did not have an attached handler nor proper training for flighted birds.
