Looks abandoned
The pictures we saw of this place on the internet before visiting showed people swimming beneath the falls. However, when we got there (with great difficulty as signage is completely absent), we found an abandoned petty waterfall and pool full of stagnant water and mosquitoes. There was a sign forbidding swimming so it must have fallen into disregard recently.
Camping and Waterfall
Nearby the waterfall is a free campground.  We liked it enough to stay three nights while visiting Seongwipo and Hallasan. The campground is a good size. The waterfall is refreshing to swim in. This place is worth checking out.
