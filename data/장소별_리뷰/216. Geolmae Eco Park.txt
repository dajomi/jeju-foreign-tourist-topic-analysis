Good for residents, less so for tourists.
As a resident of Jeju, I enjoy walking through this park every week. It is a great and safe place to take kids or walk dogs. There are two parts to the park: north of the road and south of the road. In the southern part, there are interesting ponds and pathways.I would not recommend this place to tourists only because there is so much else to see in Jeju Island that is far more impressive than this park. However, it is only a five minute walk from the city Center (Jungang Rotary) to the northern part so it is not far at all.
