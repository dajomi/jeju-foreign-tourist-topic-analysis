MUST PLACE
I visited this place during summer. As a fan of Zippo, I was AMAZED how Zippo lighters could transfer into some art. It was not only fun place for me but also a fun place for my kids. The location of the museum is fabulous!! There's a beautiful garden with a beach view right outside of the museum, which was great for my whole family. Highly recommend!
Zippo lover treasure land
If you`re a Zippo lover, this is a must visit spot. Museum locates in the ground floor of a modern seaside building with breathtaking pacific ocean view. There is  2 exhibition halls, one of those displays many special designed zippos in large size,  and the other in fact is a zippo shop, you can see and buy different limited edition zippo there and many are designed by local designer.
