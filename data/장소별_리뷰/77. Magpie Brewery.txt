Cool place to drink and eat
Magpie Brewery is a great place to have some decent western food and high quality craft beer. Their Baltic Porter is particularly good as is their Kolsch. You probably need a  car to get to the place or a taxi which will cost you ₩10,000 to ₩15,000 each way depending which part of Jeju City you are in.
Great beers
Magpie Brewery is one of the handful of craft breweries in Jeju with a great selection of seasonal and year round selections.  The pina colada ale was a tasty seasonal brew along with their classic kolsch beer.  The brewery is a bit out of the way but the outdoor picnic benches are a great spot to chill when the weather is nice.  Tours are also offered at 20,000 won.
Great for a rainy day
We were in Jeju during a thypoon event and looking for indoor activities. This was one of them and to be honest we probably would have sought out a brewery regardless of the weather. The tour was in Korean which we found out on arrival. It would have been nice to have been told that when we booked by email and before we travelled out to the brewery. However after the tour, the guide apologised and informed us that there needs to be 5 or more English speakers in the tour to do it in English which was fair enough. They then also gave us an unreleased Belgian Quadrupel to thank us. More importantly the beer was good and the pizza tasty.
