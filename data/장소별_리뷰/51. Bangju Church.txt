Quick stop on the way to Bonte Museum
Bangju Church is a picturesque little church that’s unmissable on the way to Bonte Museum.  It’s the unique ark-shaped exterior along with the surrounding water that makes it photogenic and there isn’t much inside (which is off limits most of the time anyway).  Despite being a short stop, there were always a half dozen cars in the parking lot with people busy taking photos around the church.
