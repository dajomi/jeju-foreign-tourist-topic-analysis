Jeju island bike travel.
This being my first time in Jeju Island, it was an unforgettable experience riding around the circumference of the island. The view of the ocean was breathtaking, and being on a bicycle allowed me to experience the island in a very tangible way.Honestly, it was physically very challenging for me. The first day we were riding wasn’t so bad, but on the second day, the path was constantly switching between big uphills and big downhills. On the third day, it was pouring rain, and the winds were so strong! But even though it was hard, this experience was very empowering.
