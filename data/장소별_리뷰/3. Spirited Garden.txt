Spirited Garden: A Unique Venue for all
It is no wonder why Spirited Garden is one of the top-rated tourist attractions on Jeju Island. I found it one of the best garden I have visited so far. It is basically a Bonsai tree-themed Garden decorated with many waterfalls and beautiful ponds full of colorful koi carps. Hundreds of artistic bonsai trees can be seen planted on small to large flower pots and on the ground. The bonsai trees of fruits, especially Jeju mandarin (or orange), apples are very impressive. It has seven small gardens with beautiful exotic gates, one of which is called Secret Garden and exclusively use for national and international MICE events (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions). It appears that the garden is very famous in China and other neighboring countries. The information boards show that the garden has been visited by many honored public figures notably former premiers of China, Japan, Indonesia, and others. Also, the garden has been awarded many decorations such as “KOREA Unique Venue”, “Green MICE Venue”, etc.

Not worth it
This is an honest review which I’m only leaving because we weren’t allowed to leave the garden until we promised to give it a TripAdvisor review! Usually I’m much too lazy for such antics.First things first, I think it’s admirable what Bum-young Sung (the owner) has dreamt up and achieved over fifty odd years working here. I also think he has developed very sharp entrepreneurial acumen over this time - the entry fee is 12000 won and thousands of people (us included) seem to be happy to fork this out given the decent ratings Spirited Garden has achieved.Is it worth it? I’d have to say no for the following reasons:1) the garden is actually very small (you can wonder through it in 15-20 minutes).2) even though the trees are nice to look at (debatable given how some of the trees are shackled to metal frames), there are lots of signs imploring you to see the deeper meaning and philosophical undertones of the plants and stones, many of which didn’t really make a lot of sense.3) the owner will probably come to meet you at some point which seems superficially like a nice touch, but it did feel a bit mechanical - he would then get a phone call about some new guests coming into the park and suddenly have to dash off (probably to make sure they left a good TripAdvisor review)All in all, there’s a lot of great nature to see in Jeju, especially if you want to get away from the chaos and ponder the meaning of life, but why not climb up Mt Hallasan or do an Olle trail instead, and then go and spend your 12000 won on a present for yourself 😎

As a nature lover, I fell in love with this outdoor garden   Designed and built over 50 years by Sung, Bum Young. His spirited garden is a work of love in progress. He has worked tirelessly to create a peaceful and reflective garden filled with Bonsai trees and plant species from all over the world. He has waterfalls coy ponds and sculpture spread through the park. When you’re satisfied with the tour you can treat yourself to the wonderful and healthy buffet lunch for 18,000$ won. His staff purchase fresh ingredients everyday for this beautiful and tasty meal. Throughout the park there are three places to purchase Vous les Vous merchandise as well. Feeding the huge coy is always fun for the kids as well. Today I spent over one hour with the owner himself Mr Sung who is a sweet man with a heart for serving his community with a generous heart. He has suffered 8 surgeries related to creating his garden but he is quite healthy and happy   I appreciate his great effort to bring the world the best garden I’ve ever visited

DO NOT MISS
One of the most beautiful experiences I have had traveling all over the world. Mr. Sung created a wonderful place from what was once just rocks and dirt.  He hand-polished many of the stones and built the walls and garden.  In addition to the beautiful bonsai, there are coffee shops, tea room, gift shops with hand-made items, and many water features including several ponds with carp that my son enjoyed feeding!  This garden is visited by many world leaders and dignitaries. Mr. Sung's love of nature and of humanity is evident and deeply felt as you stroll the garden.  We purchased a book that he autographed for us.  He is one of the loveliest people you will ever meet.

Awe Inspiring
You must spend a few hours in this garden if you’re on Jeju. We visited early in the morning, before the bus tours and consequently were the only tourists. Highly recommend doing this so you have the place to yourself. Do spend the time to read the story of how the gardens were created - it’s an insight into the shear dedication Mr Sung and his team have put into the place over the years. From fields to landscaped perfection over the last 50 years. The Bunjae specimens are some of the rarest in the world, and if you’re lucky you can see the gardeners forming them. Visit the tea room for a fantastic view over the entire site. Spectacular! Thank you Mr Sung for the tea and kindness you showed us. If we return to Jeju we would be sure to again visit.
