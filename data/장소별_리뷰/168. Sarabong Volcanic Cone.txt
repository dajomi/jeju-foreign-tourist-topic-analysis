Impromptu Visit
I had requested Tripadvisor to add this as an attractive as it was along the Jeju City Tour bus route. We decided at the last minute to get off at 'Sarabong Peak' as we wanted to see the lighthouse. However, we had a 10 or 15 minute uphill walk to reach the lighthouse during a cold December day but the view more than made up for the inconvenience. We only stayed for half an hour as we wanted to catch the bus back to town as it was already getting dark.
