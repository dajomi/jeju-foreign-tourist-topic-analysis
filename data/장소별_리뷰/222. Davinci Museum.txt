Just as good as the original one in Florence.
I've visited the original Da Vinci Museum in Florence in the middle of my school field trip many years ago. Since I visited fancy museums in that trip like the Louvre or Orsay, I never expected the Da Vinci Museum to be as good as those. However, it was one of the best places I've visited in the trip. Other museums only let people to look at the exhibits but those in Da Vinci was to be operated. I think touching was stronger feeling that looking. The memory is still clear.With the good memory from Florence, I also visited the one in Jeju and it was also great. I went there with my girlfriend and worried if she doesn't like it since it seems a bit for students and children. Anyway, turned out she liked its clean and pleasant facility. I think I like the one in Jeju little more since it is not in the middle of crowded city. Maybe because the one with me matters.
