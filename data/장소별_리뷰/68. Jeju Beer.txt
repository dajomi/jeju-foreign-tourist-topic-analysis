Expensive tour that only included one glass of beer
Jeju Beer Company is the biggest of the microbreweries in Jeju.  They are now the fourth biggest brewery in Korea even though the gap between the third brewery (Lotte) and the fourth is quite large.  Despite chagrin 12,000 won for a brewery tour, the slots filled up pretty quickly and we had to reserve well ahead of time.  The factory is a bit out of the way from the main road and in the middle of a rather stinky industrial area.  The pub area inside the factory is nicely decorated and a cool spot to enjoy a few drinks straight from the source even though not a lot of food is served.  The tour is well run but only includes one beer without any opportunity to taste more than one beer type.  They charge for everything like pens and coasters which I thought was a bit petty when people are driving out of the way to visit the brewery and paying a rather steep price for the tour.
