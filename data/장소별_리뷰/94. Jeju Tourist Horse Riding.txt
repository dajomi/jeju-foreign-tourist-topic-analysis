Wonderful experience riding a horse first time but.....
It was our first time riding a horse.Strict instructions were given not to make sudden noises when riding as the frightened horses will go crazy & will cause serious injury to riders.All handphones/sling bags are not allowed too.Each rider gets to choose a hat & were matched to horses according to height.The horses walked unguided on a short (about 5 mins) fenced circuit. About 100 metres near the finish point, the horses suddenly start galloping slowly.It was not easy to balance oneself on a horse holding just 2 grips on the saddle while it was galloping. We were concerned for that moment if we will fall off & was also wondering what happened to the horse. This was probably the scary part of uncertainty.It was a good experience though it was a short ride. At least now we know how it was to ride a horse.
