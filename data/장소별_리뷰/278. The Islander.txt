A cosy little souvenir shop
This shop is located along Chilsung Fashion Street, just a short 5 min walk from Dongmun Market. There is a range of souvenirs that are affordable and not found at the typical tourist shops, e.g. scented candles, dolls, organic socks, brooches, postcards, handmade key chains of fabric horse, notebooks, tote bags and posters. The quality of products are better here.
