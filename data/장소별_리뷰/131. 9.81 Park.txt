Unique and fun place for families and for any ages
I had a lot of fun riding the carts but also enjoying the beauty of Jeju. It's situated in such a beautiful location where you can view the ocean and take Instagram photos! The interior of the building was also cool. It was futuristic and very clean. The staff were friendly and helpful! I didn't play indoor games but there were many indoor games as well, so it seems like a place that you can have fun in any season. I will definitely recommend this place for any ages!  I will go back in a heartbeat when I visit Jeju next time!!
