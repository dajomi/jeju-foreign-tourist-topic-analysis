A stunning Buddhist structure unlike any other in Korea!
The Bulsari Reace Pagoda is a dome-shaped Buddhist temple built in 1998. This structure symbolizes the national hope for the peaceful reunification between South and North Korea, and it it just beautiful. There's really not much more to it than the structure itself, but it's a lovely piece of architecture that is unlike any other in Korea that I've seen. And the view of the ocean from the pagoda is worth the trip too.
