Amazing place for kids! Better than the ones in Seoul
This place still exists as of April 2019. It's an amazing place for kids and they have so many toys and so much equipment. If your kids know about Tayo the little bus, the place is magic and if they don't well it is still a pretty good kids play cafe. There's a cafe selling drinks and food. While as noted in the previous review, the staffs English isn't good but is sufficient and they try to help. We have been to this Tayo cafe and some in Seoul as well. This one is much bigger and has more and better play equipment overall. It was also way less busy than the ones in Seoul and slightly cheaper @ 12k won per child and 3k won per adult compared to 15kw won per child and 5k won per adult in Seoul. Note: Visits are capped at 2 hours (or I'm sure the kids can play there all day)Tip: Ask the staff about riding the Tayo car ride as they are not always on but when you request, a staff member will assist with it. Also the pork cutlet and rice were pretty decent for the kids (and adults) to eat.
