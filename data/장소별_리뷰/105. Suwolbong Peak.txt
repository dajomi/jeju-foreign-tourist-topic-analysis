Great viewpoint
Viewpoint on the western end of Jeju Island.  Great place for sunset observation.  High on a cliff with good views all around.  There is a weather station here.   Was here on a windy day - with field of swirling grass. To the north of the hill is a tail along the shore where it is possible to rent electric scooters.  There is also a small museum on the prehistoric settlement found in the area.
