Walking through history
I have spent years reading about WW2 history so it's a sobering experience to walk the same places as the men and women who fought and died in that conflict. Altteureu Airfield was a WW2 Japanese airbase that sits in a strategic corner of Jeju Island, midway between China and Japan. In the 1930s bombing missions were launched from here to Shanghai, later it was a training base. There are 19 remaining concrete hardened shelters sized for a Zero fighter and Jeju has done a good job with preserving the site (even though most of it is active farmland), adding a large parking lot and bathroom, and placards to explain the history and locate the structures. It's right on the Olle trail so there are many hikers passing through and it's easy to find from the shore road by car.
