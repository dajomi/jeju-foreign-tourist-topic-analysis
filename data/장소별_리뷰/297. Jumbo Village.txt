Value for money as my kids love it
50 minutes show. Is definitely worth for money as both my kids enjoyed it. There are Chinese subtitle shown on the side screen, thus I am able to understand. Perhaps we are the only foreigner there. I presumed they know we understand Chinese thus provided Chinese subtitle? I sat in the middle of the stage, in another way sometime this may not be the best place as some show like throwing basketball I can see the back of the elephant only. The ticket price is reasonable. Adult 15k, youth 12k and child 10k. There is elephant ride 18k per pax which I do not pay for it. After the show can take photo with the elephant. Do not need to come early to this place as there's nothing to do here except the elephant ride. The performance of the show is at 1030, 130, 3pm,450pm. While waiting, there have 3 little elephant trojan to ride for the kids. There has a little cafe selling pop corn and snacks.
