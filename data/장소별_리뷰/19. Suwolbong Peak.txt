Suwolbong Peak
Suwolbong peak is a part of the tuff ring which was formed due to hydro Volcanic explosion caused due to molten lava coming in contact with water. One can see the tuff formation layer by layer formed continuously throughout the cliff. Suwolbong also has a observatory tower to monitor the weather.
