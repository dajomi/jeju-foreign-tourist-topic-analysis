Fantastic
If you have no knowledge of the K-pop industry, this may not be the place for you. But, we love it. For the ticket price, things it offers is beyond our expectation. If you have a personal preference of 'which' pop star you wanna watch for the hologram show, you should check its schedule online. The ticket has a 3-hour validity (I think it's a bit short, if you really try all the stations/ experience) There are 3 levels where you could see lots of displays (music, costumes) and to try lots of fun interactive games with cutting edge technology. For instance, to dance with the stars, to shoot a picture with them, where you could actually scan your ticket and choose from the list, which star you want to pick. We stopped half way to watch the scheduled hologram show, which lasted for about 30 mins, and it was fabulous because that is the singer we really adore. After the show, we continued with our visit for a while. You can print whatever photos you snapped (if you like it) on the spot with a small fee. Likewise, you may download all the funny photos you have snapped from their website later that day. We enjoyed ourselves tremendously.
