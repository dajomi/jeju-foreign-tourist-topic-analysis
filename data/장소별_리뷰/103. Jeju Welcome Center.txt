Excellent lady staff who goes out of her way to help us......
We had alot of information to clarify & seek the tourists information centre assistance immediately on arrival in Jeju.It was about 20 minutes walk from our hotel. The Welcome Center houses the Jeju Tourists Information Centre & other offices as well.They got a young lady staff who speaks good English to attend to our queries.She was extremely helpful. We weren't very sure about certain places we wanted to go even after her guidance ( transport/directions wise) & she was so nice to give us an internet link for her to follow/communicate with us along our journey. Which she did.Certainly, she went out of her way to help us poor tourists. Talk about great service and commitment. Wow!This is the first time in our travels that we experienced such a wonderful tourist centre staff. Many thanks.

Any specific question I asked such as what bus to take or would I be interested in going a place they were able to give me insightful answers. If you don't know what to do and your hotel is close by it should definitely be worth a visit for you.
