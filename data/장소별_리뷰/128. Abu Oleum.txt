Nice easy hike
Abu Oreum was a great easy hike.  Took about 45 minutes to get to the top, hike all the way around the crater, and get back down with a 5-year-old and 7-year-old walking.  There is a trail into the crater that we didn’t take.  The climb itself from the parking lot to the top is less than 10 minutes. Has beautiful views of the countryside around it and a very pleasant walk past some cows and a small forest. It was also very easy to find and park.
