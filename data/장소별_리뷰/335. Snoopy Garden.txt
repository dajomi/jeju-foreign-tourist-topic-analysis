Expensive but great displays
With Snoopy Garden’s steep entrance fee, it initially didn’t make my itinerary.  But the good reviews were consistent and friends also highly recommended the Charles Schulz’s Peanuts themed museum and garden.  The indoor museum part was the highlight for me which had explanations about every main Peanuts character and several comic clips that span over Schulz’s 50 years of drawing them.  The old newspaper clippings, old toys, and old books were also cool to look at.  There are dozens of great photo spots which can create some major bottlenecks on crowded days.  The garden had about half a dozen themed areas (including my favorite Charlie Brown’s baseball field) that are pretty vast and would have been a lot more pleasant to walk around in cooler weather.  We skipped the cafe that charged a criminal 8,500 won for a lemonade.  I’m not sure that the high price of admission is justified but this is a well maintained attraction(which is refreshing considering the high number of run down museums and small theme parks in Jeju) and the quality of the displays are of superb quality.  Adults and children both, also fans and non-fans of Peanuts both, would enjoy the visit here.
