Great place to hike with family
We went to hike here today and it was really fun. The air was clear and the view was beautiful. We didn’t take the route to see the ponies, so our hike lasted about an hour. We walked around 5000 steps total. The stairs are well placed so it’s easy to go up, and when we went there were only about ten-twenty people in total so it was quiet and lovely. Great place to hike before or after a nice meal to digest your food!
