Nature reserve "Gotjawal" with story
Unique geographical nature reserve called Gotjawal is Jeju local language stands for forest(곶;Got) and thorn bushs on rock(자왈;jawal). The area is kept 10-15 degrees celsius throughout the year, making Summer cool and Winter warm where tropical Northern hemisphere plants and frigid Southern hemisphere plants can coexist.There are four main Gotjawal terrain in Jeju Island, this is the one in the South-West area. They also have a guided tour around this Gotjawal forest which you can explore the area with it's own story. The thing is, they only have Korean tour so far, so you might want to speak Korean or request them to open up an English tour.
