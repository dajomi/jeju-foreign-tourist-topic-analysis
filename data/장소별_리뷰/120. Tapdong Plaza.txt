Sea view
Nothing much here. The place that local playing skateboard, long board, basketball on the street. If you are a seafood lover, this is the right place to find.
Nice windy waterfront
Our hotel room overlooks this sea. Don’t think there is a way to get down but the waterfront makes for a nice breezy walk. I suppose the reason for the decoration on the barrier is to make the walk interesting and photo worthy.
Beautifu Sunset!
At the entrance of the plaza, there is tourist information center, where you can get any info related to the area. From this plaza, there are occasional festival from time to time, this time, I just went to see sunset on the ocean. Above the big waves, sunset was pretty. There is long sea-side walk, which leads to Dragon head rock.
