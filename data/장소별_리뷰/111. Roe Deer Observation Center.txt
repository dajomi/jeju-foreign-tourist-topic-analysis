Deer feeding
Our guide bought us here as my young daughter is with us and out guide thought this is something that would delight her, and he was not wrong. We spent about ten minutes feeding the deer and went off. Didn’t really explore the place as we were rushing the complete the days itinerary. Didn’t really learn much here except that we are suppose to release the food once the deer bites it, or the staff will get very worried.
Big park but not much to see
The park is very big so wear comfy hiking shoes!  We only saw 1 wild deer after walking the whole trail. There are however deer in captive just after the entrance. Not a bad place if you have the time and effort.
Few deers few visitors
We took a short walk from the nearby peace park to visit this place in October 2016. Entrance fees were nominal but visitors were few.  Looks like not a very popular tourist spot. Just as well,  we could enjoy a short walk here without the crowds. Quite a nice place to walk around although there were not so many deer. You could buy some plant food to feed the deer. The main enclosure for the deer is not that big but the park itself has other longer walking paths.  If you do not wish to walk here from the peace park, you can take the Jeju City tour bus but need to wait.
