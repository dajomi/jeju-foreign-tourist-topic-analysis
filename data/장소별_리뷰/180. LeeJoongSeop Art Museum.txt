Not too much to see
Lee Jung-Seob Art Gallery, like most government run museums in Korea, had a low display items to museum area ratio.  It is dedicated to Lee who spent the last few years of his life in Seogwipo after being raised in present-day North Korea and spending a significant part of his life in Japan.  In a three floor building it was just one room with about 20 pieces of artifacts.  Fortunately the few artifacts which consisted of postcards Lee sent his wife, his earlier sketches, letters to his children, and his palette donated by his wife were all interesting to check out.

Amazing Artist, Lukewarm Exhibit
I have a deep love and appreciation for the artist Lee Jung Seob. The museum itself  and the neighboring art-walk is a great tribute to the artist, but the exhibition itself does not do the artist that much justice. They have a great selection of his smaller works from the earlier periods but they do not house his bigger, dynamic paintings that he is known for. This visit would be a great place to take the family as the entrance fee is very manageable. The museum offers a closer look at the artists' context and life story rather than a display of his most prominent works. In short, a brilliant artist but not the best exhibition space/display method.
