Cool Museum
Kim Tschang-Yeul museum had a stylish exterior designed by Tadao Ando.  Featuring the namesake artist Kim’s sculptures and paintings, the museum is very chic for a government run museum. Kim’s trademark is the water drop, which is added to what would otherwise look like rather plain patterns painted on canvas.  The sculptures mostly contained large glass water drops.  Some of the outdoor sculptures complement Ando’s architecture very nicely.  This was an unexpectedly cool visit.
