Everything you'll ever need plus MORE!
The five-day markets in Jeju Island are fascinating places where locals converge for their everyday needs.  Everything from hundreds of different Kimchi to farm tools to live rabbits - a visitor will find anything you need.  These markets are located in various places around the island with the largest one located in Jeju city!  Mostly under cover - it was a wonderful way to explore despite a rainstorm!What struck us the most was the incredible friendliness of the Korean merchants - knowing that we were not Korean - they were patient and was very open for us to try some of their foods!  One vendor offered me some Kimchi and when she saw my reaction to the spicy-ness - she handed me a piece of pickled lotus root to help cool my mouth followed by a big smile!
