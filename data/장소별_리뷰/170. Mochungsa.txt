Mochungsa Shrine in Sarabong Park
There are 3 memorial towers, one of Kim Man Duk, civil uprising volunteers, and one of patriot at the end of Joseon dynasty. There is Kim Man Duk's grave stone, original one which moved here because of road construction in its original place. This is located in Sara-bong(hill) park. It is very low hi,, but you can enjoy beautiful sunrise on the top of hill. Even day time, I could enjoy nice view toward Jeju international port. I recommend walk around the hill first then visit Mochungsa afterwards, The Sarabong walk path is part of Olle #18. There are many locals who work out here. You can feel like a local!
