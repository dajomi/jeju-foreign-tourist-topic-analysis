A street of interesting food to experience.......
As the name implies, this street is a street of restaurants & dining. There are so many "nice" appetizing looking  restaurants as we explored the place.A very helpful "road map" at the main road entrance shows the restaurants names & types of food & exactly its location within the street.This street is nearby Seogwipo Maeil Olle Market. Seogwipo main town is quite small, so most of these "attractions" are quite close by.
