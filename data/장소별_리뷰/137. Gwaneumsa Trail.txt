The more difficult yet more rewarding trail to Hallasan peak
As I've mentioned on my review to Seongpanak Trail, I'd recommend it as the point of entry then exit through Gwaneumsa Trail. But, if you prefer a more challenging option, do the other way around. I went down from the summit through the Gwaneumsa Trail. It was the first week of April and the snow hasn't melted completely yet, making it slippery. It is very much recommended to have a walking stick, in my case, I used my tripod. The scenery in this trail was awesome. There are valleys in yellow and white. You can also find a spring water along the way. Hiking Hallasan in general solo is not a problem, regardless of which trail you take, because there are a lot of other hikers you meet along the way.
