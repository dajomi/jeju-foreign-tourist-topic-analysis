A beautiful view of Cheonjiyeon waterfalls from this Park for Free!
Chilshimni Poetry Park is part of Jeju Olle Route 6 that was easy to located as it was along the main road, Namseongjung-ro road where Park sunshine hotel or Poong Gyung Hotel were. It was a free general park with playground where the locals hang out and it can be finish in 30 minutes for less. However, the highlight of the park was the ability to have a clear view Cheonjiyeon waterfalls from a height for free! We spent some time chilling at the table and benches to appreciate the falls. There was also a stairway down to Saeyeongyo bridge. As the park was part of Jeju Olle Route 6, we went out to the main road, Namseongjung-ro road, and followed the orange/blue ribbons to Oedolgae. I have rated this park a 4 stars for the beautiful view of the Cheonjiyeon waterfalls.
